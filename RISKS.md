# Risk Register

## Risk Management Framework

This document tracks all identified risks, their mitigation strategies, and current status.

## Risk Assessment Matrix

### Probability Levels
- **High**: > 60% chance of occurrence
- **Medium**: 30-60% chance of occurrence
- **Low**: < 30% chance of occurrence

### Impact Levels
- **High**: Significant impact on project timeline, budget, or quality
- **Medium**: Moderate impact, manageable with adjustments
- **Low**: Minor impact, easily manageable

### Risk Priority
- **Critical**: High probability AND high impact
- **High**: High probability OR high impact
- **Medium**: Medium probability AND medium impact
- **Low**: Low probability AND low impact

## Risk Monitoring

### Weekly Review Process
1. Review all active risks
2. Update probability and impact assessments
3. Verify mitigation strategy effectiveness
4. Identify new risks
5. Close resolved risks

### Monthly Risk Report
- Summary of risk status changes
- New risks identified
- Risks closed
- Risk trends analysis
- Recommended actions

### Escalation Criteria
- Any risk becoming Critical priority
- Multiple High priority risks in same category
- Mitigation strategies failing
- New risks with High impact

## Risk Retirement Process

A risk is retired when:
1. The risk is no longer applicable
2. The risk has been fully mitigated
3. The project phase that included the risk has passed
4. The risk has occurred and been resolved
